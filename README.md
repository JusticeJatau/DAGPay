# **DAGPay**

## **Description**
**DAGPay** is a **DeFi** and **NFT-powered payment platform** built on the **BlockDAG network**, designed to deliver **fast, low-cost, and inclusive digital finance** for individuals, small businesses, and organizations.  
It enables **instant payments, escrow transactions**, and **digital e-cheques** using **DAG Coin** as its native currency — accessible even via **USSD** for non-smartphone users.

---

## **Project Information**
Despite the growth of digital finance, over **1.7 billion adults** worldwide remain excluded from formal financial systems — particularly across **Africa** and **emerging markets**.  
Existing solutions face multiple critical challenges:

### **High Transaction Costs**
Traditional payment and remittance services charge fees between **5–10%**, making small or cross-border transfers expensive and inefficient.

### **Currency Instability**
Local currencies in many developing countries, such as the **Naira**, suffer from rapid devaluation and inflation, destroying savings and reducing purchasing power.

### **Limited Banking Infrastructure**
Millions of people and small businesses remain **unbanked or underbanked**, unable to access financial services, credit, or secure payment tools.

### **Exclusion from Digital Finance**
Most blockchain and fintech solutions rely on **smartphones** and **internet access**, excluding feature phone users who make up a large share of the population.

### **Lack of Trust and Transparency**
In B2B and government transactions, **payment fraud**, **delays**, and **corruption** are widespread due to the absence of transparent, verifiable payment systems.

### **Crypto Utility Gap**
Although crypto adoption is increasing rapidly, there remains a **lack of real-world use cases** connecting digital assets to everyday activities like **payments, salaries, and remittances**.

## **How It Works**
**DAGPay** provides a unified **DeFi** and **NFT-powered** financial ecosystem built on the **BlockDAG protocol**, enabling **fast, transparent, and inclusive digital payments** using **DAG Coin** as the native currency.

### **Here’s how it solves the problem:**

#### **Fast & Low-Cost Transactions**
Leverages the **BlockDAG consensus** to process thousands of transactions per second with near-zero fees — enabling **instant payments** and **cross-border transfers**.

#### **Inclusive Financial Access (USSD Integration)**
Users without internet access or smartphones can **send and receive DAG Coin via USSD codes**, bridging the gap for the unbanked and underbanked population.

#### **Stable and Transparent Digital Currency**
**DAG Coin** serves as a **stable, low-volatility medium of exchange**, protecting users from inflation and maintaining transparency on-chain.

#### **DeFi Features**
Users can **stake, lend, or borrow assets** directly within the platform, creating new **income opportunities** without a traditional bank.

#### **NFT-Based Digital Identity & Receipts**
Transactions, contracts, and ownership proofs are **tokenized as NFTs**, ensuring authenticity, traceability, and preventing fraud in digital agreements.

#### **Government & Business Adoption**
Supports **escrow smart contracts** and **verifiable e-cheques**, enabling secure transactions between individuals, businesses, and government bodies.

#### **Multi-Platform Access**
Available as a **mobile app, web app, and USSD service**, ensuring accessibility for users across all devices and regions.

## **Target Group**

### **Unbanked & Underbanked Individuals**
- Rural and urban populations in emerging markets without access to traditional banking.  
- Users with feature phones who need financial services via USSD.

### **SMEs and Micro-Businesses**
- Small and medium-sized enterprises requiring **low-cost payments, escrow, and cross-border transactions**.  
- Businesses seeking **on-chain verification** and **payment automation**.

### **Organizations & Public Institutions**
- Ministries, social welfare programs, and salary disbursement systems requiring **transparent, traceable, and instant payments**.

### **Crypto Users**
- Individuals already holding cryptocurrencies seeking **real-world payment utility** and **stable transactional mediums**.

### **Diaspora & Remittance Senders**
- International users sending money to family and businesses in emerging markets at **low fees** and **near-instant settlement**.
